# Entry #2
Chapter 2 of _The Design of Everyday Things_ talks about how people tend to approach problems and how to keep that in mind when designing. The very first step is forming the goal. The three steps after are the execution, the person plans their action, specifies how they're going to do it, then performs the actions. Th last three steps are all about responding to the outcome, you perceive the state of the world after your action, interpret what the outcome means, and compare the outcome with the goal. If the outcome does not line up with the goal you had in mine, you start the process over again. You want to minimize how often this loop has to be repeated by the person, making sure that from the beginning the person is able to come up with the plan that is intended with what you designed.
